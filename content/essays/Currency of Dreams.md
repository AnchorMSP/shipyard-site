“Dream”: [4a] A strongly desired goal or purpose.

Since the dawn of time, currency has been the driving factor of a growing and/or thriving society. 

Both the abundance and movement are decisive of its value. And often this currency is a material of intrinsic value or a material signifying something with said intrinsic value.

And I would like to propose as an idea, an observation. 

The true currency driving our innermost being is dreams. The dream of success, of love, of joy, of wholeness.

Outside of truly basic needs, we as humans seem to use money, time, resources, services as mere representations of the dreams we strive for and the receivers often time doing the same in their own right.

May we, dear fellow traversers of this confusing existence continue to strive towards our dreams, whilst allowing others to do the same.
