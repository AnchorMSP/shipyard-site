"Desire": A strong feeling of wanting to have something, achieve a goal.

Simple an observation today dear reader.

The complexities of our very existence seem to drive us to constantly explore and create, specifically that which is beyond us. 

Possibly an attempt at escape from the reality we abide in? Possibly the nature of growth itself. But we as a species seem to strive to explore that which we don't understand create that which supersedes our very own capabilities and understanding.


May we, dear fellow traversers of this confusing existence both continue to allow ourselves to create and explore beyond ourselves whilst granting our innate desire to simply abide in the life and reality we have been placed in.
