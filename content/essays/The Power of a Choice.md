"Choice": The act of choosing.

Often times the outcomes of our interactions in this world are solely determined based on choices we do or don't make. 

Rarely do we realize in these moments that the said choice is the deciding factor in our immediate or distant future. 

Now don't get me wrong, occasionally we do NOT have a choice, but that seems to be the minority. 

The choice to connect, the choice to be silent, the choice to ask a question, the choice to try, etc. are truly opportunities we are given, day in and day out to guide our own path.

May we, dear fellow traversers of this confusing existence, continue to choose the things that will provide value, establish being, and bring wisdom to this reality we call home.
